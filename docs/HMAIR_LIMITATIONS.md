# HMAIR H1 Limitations

**Status:** H1 normative limitation record, updated 2026-09-11.
H1 is **Accepted at 12 of 12**. This document describes what the accepted H1
substrate cannot establish and must not be used as evidence that later HMAIR
collectors, path-native proof, coverage, user experience, or scale work is
complete.

## 1. Explicit unsupported conclusions

H1 does not prove:

- an AI asset exists merely because a target or collector is configured;
- installed software was used;
- configured reachability was exercised;
- Hawk can authorize, prove, or contain a path;
- an unknown candidate is shadow AI;
- collector breadth, source-complete asset inventory, UI completion, or
  enterprise scale.

The unsupported conclusions above are exact H1 boundaries. A target,
collector, assignment, health report, dry-run, or scan lifecycle record is
orchestration metadata. None is a substitute for sanitized source evidence,
canonical resolution, observed activity, verified identity attribution,
executed reachability, inline enforcement, or retained proof.

## 2. Conservative evidence semantics

H1 persists immutable, provenance-bearing observations. An observation is a
source claim; it is not automatically a canonical AI Asset or an accepted
governance conclusion. H1 therefore follows these rules:

- no evidence means **Unknown**, not absent, safe, protected, or unused;
- unsupported source depth remains **Unsupported**, not successful empty
  coverage;
- missing permissions remain **Permission denied**, not proof that the source
  contains no assets;
- stale evidence remains **Stale**, not current estate state;
- a configured or installed state is not observed use;
- configured, observed, inferred, simulated, blocked, and unknown reachability
  remain distinct;
- collector health is not permission, asset existence, source breadth,
  authorization capability, or proof capability; and
- a partial or cancelled scan cannot be presented as complete coverage.

Customer-visible percentages, confidence bands, or Control Coverage claims
must not be inferred from H1 orchestration counts. Coverage remains unknown or
not covered unless the later authoritative service has measurable telemetry
and retained evidence for the specific asset and path.

## 3. Current H1 substrate boundary

The accepted H1 evidence recorded in the implementation plan covers all twelve
checklist items: contract vocabularies; canonical scan/target/collector/
observation models; the Collector SDK boundary; privacy-safe ingestion; bounded
scan orchestration; tenant-safe APIs; canonical assets, fingerprints,
capabilities, Evidence Claims, resolution decisions and reversible history;
usage, attribution, reachability, and change-event persistence; asset detail,
evidence, capability, context, and history reads; bounded compatibility
summaries; and default-off configuration. The actual linear H1 asset and
attribution revisions are `0055` through `0058`; the historical `0048` and
`0049` labels were already occupied by other migrations.

H1 still does not establish:

- a native active collector or any claim of collector surface breadth;
- scan or asset user-experience pages, or an operational scan workflow;
- that a configured collector has produced a canonical asset or that a
  canonical asset inventory is complete for any deployment surface;
- source-backed usage, identity, or reachability for a customer merely because
  the corresponding persistence and read contracts exist;
- asset-detail UI, scan-result UI, or a complete enterprise workflow;
- Control Coverage enrichment, authorization, containment, findings, or graph
  projections from accepted HMAIR claims;
- continuous discovery, retention/compaction operations, representative
  high-cardinality benchmarks, or enterprise-scale acceptance; or
- proof that any concrete deployment surface is discoverable, inline,
  monitor-only, bypass-aware, or governed.

## 4. H2 entry boundary

The shared substrate prerequisites for H2 are satisfied. H1 closure includes a
fresh PostgreSQL 16 upgrade/downgrade/re-upgrade gate, focused H1 and legacy
compatibility tests, literal compilation of changed Python files, the sole
repository-head check, and a protected-file/secret review. The pre-existing
`UU` database paths remain unresolved but retain the same recorded Git objects
and were not opened, changed, staged, resolved, or migrated by H1 closure.

H2 must now prove real endpoint, browser, and MCP/LLM observation paths,
cross-surface resolution, source-backed attribution and reachability,
conservative coverage, findings, and product drill-down. Enabling flags,
registering a declaration, or returning an empty scan cannot satisfy H2.

## 5. Known blind spots

Because no active collector is enabled by H1, all enterprise source surfaces
remain unproven at this checkpoint. Passive evidence is limited to data that a
separately accepted integration has already delivered under its own contract.
H1 makes no completeness claim for endpoint, browser, cloud, Kubernetes,
container, MCP, LLM gateway, SaaS, identity, repository, CI/CD, network, DNS,
API gateway, database, vector, search, RAG, prompt, memory, or knowledge
surfaces.

Blind spots must remain visible as unknown or unsupported until source-specific
evidence closes them. Hawk must not infer protection from configuration,
deployment family, connector registration, source health, target scope, or the
absence of observations.

## 6. H4 source-and-fixture boundary

H4 now defines and tests the required provider and surface breadth through 31
catalog-backed, passive metadata profiles. This establishes supported typed
projection behavior, privacy rejection, target/surface gating, and operational
documentation. It does not establish that any particular customer has enabled,
connected, authorized, or completed those sources.

Direct Microsoft 365 and GitHub collection is available only through their
existing typed source contracts. Every other provider requires an existing
approved customer connector or an attested metadata feed. If the source cannot
supply an authoritative denominator and a fresh completion receipt, coverage
remains partial or Unknown. No profile proves active use, inline control,
business outcome, vulnerability status, certification, or complete enterprise
discovery.

## 7. H5 source-and-fixture boundary

H5 now defines and tests the exact governance vocabularies, evidence-backed
projection rules, accepted-claim graph admission, seven-dimension discovery
coverage, closed finding/change-event catalogs, and bounded incremental rescan
planning. These are deterministic projections over existing canonical records;
they do not independently prove that a customer source is connected, complete,
current, or capable of inline control.

An installed or configured asset is not active use. A configured or inferred
route is not observed reachability. Approved-but-uncontrolled remains distinct
from approved and controlled. Missing identity, ownership, denominator,
evidence, authorization, containment, or bypass proof remains Unknown or Not
Covered. Incremental work is limited to assets with changed canonical digests
and current source provenance; an absent provenance link does not trigger an
unbounded estate rescan.

H5 acceptance does not establish production deployment, live customer-source
completion, authoritative organization denominators, or H6 release acceptance.
H6 now adds bounded retention/compaction and application-layer estate-scale
benchmark evidence; it does not turn those source-and-fixture results into a
connected-customer or complete-enterprise claim.
