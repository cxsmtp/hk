## Evidence rules

The machine-readable result is `docs/evidence/gemini-cli-0.54.4-acceptance-result.json`; its SHA-256 is bound by `docs/evidence/gemini-cli-acceptance-registry.json`. A registry entry verifies only its explicit capability list. It does not promote the full adapter, other versions, direct Gemini APIs, Gemini web surfaces, or an unhealthy/tampered installation.

The local acceptance run also rendered and tamper-tested a hash-pinned managed launcher and verified the staged binary's exact version. That proves the deployable artifact, not MDM delivery. Enterprise Admin Controls and MDM deployment remain unclaimed until their separate tenant-bound signed receipts pass Hawk's authoritative verifier.
